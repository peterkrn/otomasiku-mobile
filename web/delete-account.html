Delete Otomasiku Account

Guide to request deletion of your account and data

Steps to delete your account:

1

Open the Otomasiku app

Open the Otomasiku app on your Android device

2

Go to Profile

Tap the Profile icon in the bottom navigation

3

Open Settings

Select the Settings menu

4

Select Delete Account

Scroll to the bottom and select "Delete Account"

5

Confirm deletion

Read the information carefully, check the confirmation box, and tap "Delete My Account"

What happens to your data?

Deleted Immediately

  • • User profile and personal information
  • • Saved shipping addresses
  • • Saved payment methods
  • • Shopping cart items
  • • Projects, favorites, and product bookmarks

Retained Temporarily (30 days)

  • • Order history and transactions
  • • Payment records
  • This data will be permanently deleted after 30 days for legal and tax compliance

Permanently Retained

  • • Order history and transaction records (anonymized)
  • • Payment records (anonymized)
  • • Anonymized sales statistics for business analytics
  • All personal identifiable information is removed. Retained for legal, tax, and accounting compliance requirements as required by Indonesian law.
